# Universal Character Contract

Every standard fighter has:
- 15 normals: 5L/5M/5H, 2L/2M/2H, 6L/6M/6H, 4L/4M/4H, jL/jM/jH.
- 15 specials: Neutral/Forward/Back/Down/Up × Light/Medium/Heavy.
- Forward throw, back throw, air throw, throw whiff, and throw-tech reactions.
- One primary ultimate.

Functional coverage must include a low, overhead, anti-air, launcher, advancing normal, defensive/retreating normal, air combo tool, air finisher, and counter-hit reward.

Universal systems include movement, dash/backdash, jump/super-jump, forward/back air dash, standing/crouching/air block, Perfect Guard, metered Guard Cancel, separate Burst resource, chain/cancel rules, pursuit follow-up, counter-hit, clash where declared, hitstop, hitstun, blockstun, wall/ground bounce, crumple, stagger, knockdowns, scaling, air/ground recovery, and wake-up rules.

The engine distinguishes standard throw, air throw, command grab, anti-air command grab, hit-grab, and cinematic grab.

Swahili is the first command-grab fighter. His command grabs are special moves, normally untechable, and require explicit target eligibility, startup, capture, release, whiff recovery, armor/invulnerability, combo, paired animation, and camera rules.

Character packages describe data/assets and must not patch engine internals to exist.
