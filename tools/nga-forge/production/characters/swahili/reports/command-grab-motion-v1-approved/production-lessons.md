# Swahili Command Grab Motion V1 - Production Lessons

- Lock the physical action order before polishing connectors.
- Preserve approved frames and make additive, candidate-only corrections.
- Treat the canonical scythe as a rigid prop; only position, rotation, and approved perspective projection may change.
- Sell weapon weight with body load, weapon lag, acceleration spacing, impact compression, and follow-through.
- Use the blade path as the motion spine for the scoop, carry, side switch, and release.
- Keep victim-root and attacker-root paths explicit so the side switch cannot read as teleportation.
- Separate review playback speed from authoritative gameplay timing and balance.
- Repair isolated weapon geometry or individual frames without reopening approved body art.
- Validate authored and mirrored behavior, frame scrub, hashes, source provenance, and locked runtime boundaries.
