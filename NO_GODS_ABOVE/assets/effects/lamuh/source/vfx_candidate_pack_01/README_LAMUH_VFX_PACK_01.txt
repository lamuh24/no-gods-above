LAMUH VFX CANDIDATE PACK 01

Purpose
- Candidate VFX spritesheets for LAMUH in No Gods Above.
- These are art/source candidates for Codex to evaluate and hook up.
- Preserve transparency/alpha when importing.

Important style rule
- Divine Vanish is NOT part of this gold/cyan pack.
- Keep Divine Vanish's existing purple phase/afterimage identity.
- Do not overwrite Divine Vanish with the gold/cyan VFX from this pack.

Suggested usage mapping
1) lamuh_vfx_01_celestial_palm_projectile_candidate.png
   Best for: Celestial Palm projectile / regular ki blast / orb startup
   Notes: clean orb-to-projectile progression.

2) lamuh_vfx_02_charge_fire_impact_candidate.png
   Best for: charge-up, palm-fire burst, impact burst, mini super-charge moments
   Notes: useful for charge/fire/impact language around beam or strong palm shots.

3) lamuh_vfx_03_dash_dive_impact_candidate.png
   Best for: Ascend Step trail, Radiant Dive trail, fast strike bursts, impact slashes
   Notes: includes forward velocity shapes, dive-like motion, and impact bursts.

4) lamuh_vfx_04_heaven_splitter_vertical_candidate.png
   Best for: Heaven Splitter, vertical anti-air eruptions, downward/landing pillar hits
   Notes: strongest vertical column language.

5) lamuh_vfx_05_column_slash_impact_candidate.png
   Best for: alternate Heaven Splitter/Radiant Dive impact, heavy hit bursts, landing spikes
   Notes: more dramatic vertical and impact shapes.

6) lamuh_vfx_06_misc_ring_portal_burst_candidate.png
   Best for: utility accents, spawn rings, teleport-adjacent rings, charge circles, accent bursts
   Notes: optional support sheet; use selectively.

7) lamuh_vfx_07_crown_of_no_gods_beam_candidate.png
   Best for: Crown of No Gods beam body / beam growth / sustain / taper
   Notes: this is the beam candidate generated here that was preferred visually.

Recommended move language
- Celestial Palm: use VFX 01 primarily; optionally accent with 02.
- Ascend Step: use 03 primarily.
- Heaven Splitter: use 04 primarily; 05 as alternate or impact.
- Divine Vanish: keep existing purple effect. Do not replace from this pack.
- Radiant Dive: use 03 for travel and 04 or 05 for landing impact.
- Crown of No Gods: use 07 for beam, 02 for charge/fire accents, and 04/05 for impact if needed.
