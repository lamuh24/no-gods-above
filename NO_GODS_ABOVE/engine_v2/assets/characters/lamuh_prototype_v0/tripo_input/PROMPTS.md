# Locked Tripo Prompts

## Main character prompt

Create a clean, fully modeled, stylized anime fighting-game character in a neutral A-pose using all supplied turnaround views as strict identity references. Lamuh is a powerful Black male celestial martial artist with dark brown skin, a stern mature face, short shaped beard and mustache, and very high-volume thick black locs radiating outward in a distinctive wide silhouette. Preserve his long structured white coat with gold trim and split tails, fitted black inner top, loose black martial-arts pants, saturated teal waist sash with a hanging tail, centered gold pendant, and gold-and-teal open sandals. His build is tall, broad-shouldered, powerful, athletic, and heroic—not bodybuilder exaggerated. Use clean stylized anatomy, readable hands and feet, animation-friendly joint loops, strong silhouette, and production-ready separation of body, coat, inner clothes, pants, sash, loc hair, sandals, and pendant. Match the supplied front, side, back, three-quarter, face, and costume details exactly. Neutral expression. Symmetrical A-pose. No weapon. No VFX. No environment. No base. No baked lighting. No animation. No root motion.

## Negative prompt

Do not make a generic cyberpunk character, sci-fi armor, robot, mannequin, monk robe, samurai armor, superhero suit, photoreal human, chibi, child, elderly man, pale skin, straight hair, short locs, thin silhouette, shaved face, hood, cape replacing the coat, blue jeans, sneakers, boots, metallic bodysuit, neon circuitry, weapons, wings, horns, crown, aura, energy effects, floor base, or background. Do not merge the coat, sash, locs, sandals, or pendant into the body. Do not alter facial identity, skin tone, proportions, palette, gold trim pattern, teal sash, or outward loc silhouette.

## Accessory correction prompt

Refine only the accessories while preserving the approved body and face: centered gold celestial pendant on a simple chain; saturated teal sash wrapped once at the waist with one readable hanging tail; gold-and-teal open sandals with visible toes and ankle straps; clean continuous gold edging on the white coat. Keep each accessory as a separate named object. Do not add jewelry, armor, weapons, or extra ornament.

## Hair correction prompt

Refine only Lamuh's hair while preserving the approved body, face, and costume: thick black locs with large volume, spreading outward and slightly upward from the scalp in an unmistakable wide halo-like silhouette. Locs must have varied length and natural grouping while remaining animation-friendly and separated from the coat collar. Do not use braids, an afro, straight hair, short dreads, ponytail, hood, beads, glowing tips, or physics intersections.
