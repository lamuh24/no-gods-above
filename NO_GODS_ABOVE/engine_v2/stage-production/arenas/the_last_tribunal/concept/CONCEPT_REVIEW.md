## Review board

`concept_composition_board_v1.png` is a concept-only eight-panel board covering the wide establishing view, standard gameplay camera, both corners, jump height, command grab, super/ultimate wide, and intro/victory framing. It was generated with the built-in image-generation route and copied into the arena review package at SHA-256 `E8921A73260B9894666A6CFF1962CDFDECBF998ABA46592C1E398D83C4587958`.

The board is not a texture source and is never loaded by the graybox or runtime.

## Direction retained

- High central eclipse/oculus above the standard fighter-head band.
- Broad dark value grouping through the combat lane.
- Flat matte floor with a restrained radial judgment motif.
- Empty gallery depth and a solemn post-divine institutional silhouette.
- Cathedral-red vertical accents outside the fighter contrast zone.
- Consistent arena identity across gameplay and cinematic framings.

## Graybox corrections to the generated board

- Statues, trim, and gallery micro-detail were reduced substantially behind fighters.
- Brass luminance was lowered so Swahili's gold costume accents remain dominant.
- Floor line density was reduced to two rings and three restrained longitudinal markers.
- Foreground ornaments were replaced by simple edge pillars outside the core lane.
- The oculus was raised and validated numerically against gameplay, corner, jump, and cinematic frames.

Exact Swahili, bright/dark placeholder, beam, projectile, hit-spark, and muzzle-flash contrast was evaluated in browser graybox captures rather than generated character imagery.
