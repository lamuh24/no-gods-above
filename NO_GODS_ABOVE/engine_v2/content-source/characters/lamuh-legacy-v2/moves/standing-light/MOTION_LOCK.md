# Lamuh Legacy V2 Standing Light Motion Lock

Status: `V1_MOTION_LOCKED_FOR_TARGETED_V2_SINGLE_HIT_REBUILD`

This lock protects the useful V1 motion while documenting the one place that needs modernization. The four legacy cells preserve a strong wide stance, a compact lead-arm straight, a guarded compression, coat/loc lag, and a quick neutral return. They also show two full-extension silhouettes in a move that registers one hit.

The V2 candidate therefore preserves the source poses as motion evidence but authors one continuous six-frame action:

1. compact ready entry
2. lead-fist chamber
3. single straight contact
4. same-arm follow-through
5. lead-arm recoil
6. connected guard recovery

Only frame 02 may show contact VFX. The renderer uses one fixed scale and root. The original V1 PNGs remain hash-locked and unchanged.

Timing candidates remain independent: A reconstructs the 15-tick V1 rhythm, B adds readability to anticipation/follow-through/recoil for 18 ticks, and C is a 20-tick weighted alternate. Human review must approve motion and timing separately.
