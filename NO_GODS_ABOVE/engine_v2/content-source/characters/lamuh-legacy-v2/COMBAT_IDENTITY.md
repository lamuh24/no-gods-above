# Lamuh Legacy V2 combat identity — candidate

Current 2026-09-05 implementation: nine normals, universal forward/back throws, and four L/M/H special families: Ascend Step, detached Celestial Palm projectiles, Heaven Splitter uppercuts, and candidate Radiant Dive air approaches. Forward specials remain accepted-for-now/starred; Palm and Heaven (including Heavy V4 aura) passed their scoped family reviews. Radiant Dive now supplies one descending open-palm strike with shallow/diagonal/steep travel, authored Heavy surrounding aura and actual-floor landing recovery. It remains pending human motion/combat review. Lamuh stays a mobile close-to-midrange confirm fighter with committed ranged, anti-air and air-approach tools; Divine Vanish and ultimate are not implemented. See the [newest special-family checkpoint](SPECIAL_FAMILY_REVIEW_2026-09-05.md) and [Radiant source/design audit](RADIANT_DIVE_REVIEW_2026-09-05.md). Earlier benchmark snapshots do not describe current coverage.

Status: **PENDING HUMAN APPROVAL**. This proposal preserves the strongest identity supported by the legacy kit and does not redefine him as Swahili.

- Preferred range: close-to-mid range, with a light projectile check and burst movement to enter.
- Mobility: above-average grounded and air mobility; momentum is part of the character, not cosmetic drift.
- Pressure style: short, readable strings into a small legacy-special family; deliberate recovery prevents unstructured spam.
- Combo identity: body-driven normals into Ascend Step/Heaven Splitter routes, with launch and air follow-up rather than long autonomous sequences.
- Defensive strengths: evasive repositioning and reliable air control.
- Primary weaknesses: whiff recovery, linear committed movement, modest damage without confirmed routes, and limited long-range control.
- Meter usage: movement extension, confirm conversion, and defensive reset; ultimate deferred until the core fighter passes review.
- Signature movement: forward commitment, rising attack arcs, sharp directional redirection, and continuous coat/hair follow-through.
- Signature attack qualities: readable anticipation, fast strike acceleration, exposed impact, connected recovery, restrained squash/stretch, and clean silhouettes.

## Proposed concise V2 identity

A mobile celestial rush fighter who wins by carrying body momentum through short confirms and legacy signature movement. He should feel quick and fluid, but not frictionless: the strike remains fast while anticipation, contact, follow-through, and recovery are readable.
