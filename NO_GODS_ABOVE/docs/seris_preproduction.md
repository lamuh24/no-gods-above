# Seris Pre-Production Brief

Seris is the selected next new-generation fighter concept for NO GODS ABOVE after Nyx is fully complete.

This is a pre-production planning document only. Do not generate Seris sprites, add runtime code, start controller support, redesign combat, or modify Kairo/Vanta from this brief unless explicitly requested later.

## Roster Gap Analysis

Current roster coverage:

- Kairo: stable legacy blade striker with blue/white cyber blade identity.
- Vanta: stable legacy heavier rival with red/black pressure identity.
- Nyx: first new-generation fighter, fast purple/magenta aerial rushdown and shadow assassin identity.

The roster is missing a true mid-range control fighter: someone who owns space without becoming a projectile zoner, slows Nyx-style pressure down, and brings a distinct silhouette and color language.

## Candidate Concepts

### Candidate 1 - Seris

- Name: Seris
- Fantasy: A celestial chain duelist who carves the screen with floating halo-blades.
- Archetype: Mid-range chain / whip fighter.
- Profile: Medium health, medium damage, medium speed.
- Movement: Controlled ground movement, average dash, clean air dash, deliberate jump arcs.
- Combo style: Ground pokes into chain confirms, launcher from chain pull, air follow-ups with sweeping arcs.
- Strengths: Strong mid-range buttons, good anti-rushdown, stylish confirms, readable VFX.
- Weaknesses: Slower up close, punishable whiffs, weaker scramble defense.
- Visual identity: Elegant armored executioner with orbiting chain segments and crescent blades.
- Color identity: Ivory, black, turquoise, pale gold.
- Roster value: Adds space control and elegant weapon mastery without overlapping Nyx's purple aerial rushdown.

### Candidate 2 - Bront

- Name: Bront
- Fantasy: A meteor-knuckled bruiser who walks through pressure and detonates at close range.
- Archetype: Armored pressure bruiser / close-range brawler.
- Profile: High health, high damage, low-medium speed.
- Movement: Heavy grounded dash, short jump, strong forward momentum.
- Combo style: Shorter routes, big impact hits, launcher from uppercut-style armor breaker.
- Strengths: High reward, intimidating pressure, simple game plan, strong comeback feel.
- Weaknesses: Can be outspaced, less air control, vulnerable to whiff punishment.
- Visual identity: Massive gauntlets, cracked obsidian armor, impact craters and ember bursts.
- Color identity: Charcoal, molten orange, steel, white impact flashes.
- Roster value: Adds a tankier close-range power character, but risks visual overlap with Vanta's red/black heaviness.

### Candidate 3 - Ivara

- Name: Ivara
- Fantasy: A gravity dancer who bends spacing with star-rings and delayed orbit traps.
- Archetype: Gravity / space manipulator.
- Profile: Low-medium health, medium damage, medium speed.
- Movement: Floaty jumps, unique-looking air drift, standard air dash compatibility.
- Combo style: Trap confirms into launcher, delayed VFX hits, juggle extensions.
- Strengths: Distinct identity, high style, unusual neutral control.
- Weaknesses: Harder to implement cleanly, risks needing new combat logic, harder to balance.
- Visual identity: Astral monk with ring-shaped gravity sigils and suspended cloth panels.
- Color identity: Deep green, silver, black, star-white.
- Roster value: Visually fresh and mechanically exciting, but more likely to strain the current system.

## Recommended Choice

Seris, the Halo Chain, is the recommended next production concept.

She expands the roster with mid-range control while staying compatible with the current FighterZ-inspired system. She can use normal hitboxes, character-specific move data, chain VFX, launcher routes, jump cancels, air combos, and an ultimate without requiring a combat redesign. Her ivory, turquoise, gold, and black identity also separates her from Kairo, Vanta, and Nyx.

## Core Identity

- Name: Seris
- Title: The Halo Chain
- Fantasy: An elegant celestial executioner who fights with chained crescent blades orbiting her like a weaponized halo.
- Personality: Calm, severe, graceful, almost priestly. She does not rush. She judges.

## Gameplay Profile

- Archetype: Mid-range chain / whip fighter.
- Health: 960, sturdier than Nyx but not a bruiser.
- Damage: Medium, with high reward on clean confirms.
- Speed: Medium ground speed, average dash, average air dash.
- Movement feel: Precise and measured rather than frantic.
- Difficulty: Medium.
- Pressure style: Uses chain normals to hold the opponent at ideal range, then converts with pull-in or launcher routes.
- Neutral style: Controls the middle of the screen with long but punishable crescent-chain attacks.
- Combo style: Ground poke confirm into chain pull or launcher, then jump cancel into air chain finisher.

## Move List

### Light Attack - Halo Jab

A quick short-range palm-and-chain flick.

- Used for: Close-range starts when enemies get inside her ideal range.
- Fits because: Seris needs a clean defensive starter without becoming a rushdown fighter.

### Medium Attack - Crescent Lash

A forward chain slash with better reach than standard normals.

- Used for: Mid-range confirms and pressure checks.
- Fits because: The weapon identity becomes clear immediately.

### Heavy Attack - Judgment Sweep

A wide arcing chain swing with meaningful recovery.

- Used for: Punishing movement and threatening space.
- Fits because: It sells her calm, deliberate executioner feel.

### Launcher - Ascension Hook

The chain snaps upward and lifts the opponent.

- Used for: Jump-cancel combo routes and anti-ground conversions.
- Fits because: The halo-chain fantasy literally hoists enemies into the air.

### Air Light - Falling Link

A fast downward chain tap.

- Used for: Starting air strings after launcher.
- Fits because: It keeps her air route controlled, not Nyx-fast.

### Air Medium - Moonring Cut

A horizontal crescent slash in the air.

- Used for: Stabilizing juggles and carrying the opponent.
- Fits because: It reinforces the orbiting blade motif.

### Air Heavy - Bellfall Cleave

A heavier descending chain-blade strike that ends air routes with knockdown.

- Used for: Main air finisher.
- Fits because: It gives her air combos an executioner finish.

### Special 1 - Chain Recall

Seris throws a crescent chain forward. On hit, it pulls the opponent slightly inward for a combo confirm.

- Used for: Converting mid-range pokes.
- Fits because: It supports control play without requiring a new grab system.

### Special 2 - Halo Step

A short evasive reposition with chain afterimages, ending at a controlled angle rather than crossing up like Nyx.

- Used for: Resetting spacing or avoiding predictable pressure.
- Fits because: It expresses elegant, measured movement.

### Special 3 - Sanctum Snare

A brief ground-level chain sigil appears in front of Seris and strikes after a short delay.

- Used for: Pressure, setup timing, and forcing movement.
- Fits because: It adds judge/trap identity while staying simpler than a full puppet character.

### Signature / Ultimate - Heaven's Guillotine

A giant luminous chain halo opens behind Seris, then a massive crescent blade drops diagonally across the screen with ivory, turquoise, and gold VFX.

- Used for: Flashy super-style visual and combo finisher.
- Fits because: It makes Seris feel like a real fighting game character with a marketable signature move.

## Visual Identity

- Silhouette: Tall, elegant, asymmetric, with floating chain arcs around the shoulders and back.
- Outfit: Ritual combat coat, fitted armor plates, long split cloth, sharp crescent motifs.
- Hair / head: Pale hair or veil-like headpiece, clean face silhouette, small halo-chain crown.
- Body language: Upright, composed, one hand guiding chains like a conductor.
- Palette: Ivory armor, black underlayer, turquoise energy, pale gold highlights.
- VFX: Crescent arcs, chain links, halo rings, white-hot blade edges, turquoise-gold sparks.
- Difference from roster: Not Kairo's cyber blue, not Vanta's red heavy rival look, and not Nyx's purple shadow assassin identity. Seris should read as bright, sacred, precise, and weapon-master elegant.

## Production Readiness

Sprite sheet categories needed later:

- Core movement.
- Air movement.
- Ground normals.
- Air normals.
- Specials.
- Defense / hit reactions.
- Knockdown / recovery / flavor.
- Signature / ultimate VFX.
- Select portrait/card.

Likely VFX needs:

- Chain slash trails.
- Crescent arcs.
- Pull-in chain effect.
- Ground snare sigil.
- Halo Step afterimage.
- Heaven's Guillotine ultimate slash.

Select screen direction:

- Seris in a calm execution pose.
- Chain halo open behind her.
- One crescent blade angled forward.
- Ivory/turquoise/gold card accent.
- Role label: Mid-Range Chain.

Pipeline risks:

- Chain links can become visually noisy in sprite sheets.
- Long weapon arcs must avoid frame-edge clipping.
- Ultimate VFX needs large cells like Nyx's slash-wave profile, not projectile trimming.

Implementation advantages:

- Can be built through character-specific config values.
- Can use existing launcher, jump-cancel, air-combo, and air-dash rules.
- Can use current special-move and VFX patterns.
- Can follow Nyx-style atlas production without redesigning combat or touching Kairo/Vanta.

## Production Gate

Do not build Seris until explicitly requested after Nyx is complete enough to move forward. When production starts, follow the Nyx pipeline:

1. Lock gameplay identity.
2. Lock move list.
3. Lock visual identity.
4. Generate sprite sheets.
5. Audit and fix sheets.
6. Extract/import atlases.
7. Create character select image.
8. Run focused playtest.
9. Run normal matchup tests against Nyx and legacy fighters.
