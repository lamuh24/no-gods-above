## Critical Findings Before More Sprite Sheets

1. Reactions and defensive states are entirely old/final-generation art: block high, block low, air block, light/medium/heavy hitstun, launch hit, knockback, knockdown, downed, get-up, KO, intro/victory/taunt. This explains the broad visual inconsistency during normal gameplay.
2. `special_recovery` still points to old Sheet 5 Row 5 and is returned after many redesigned specials in `getLamuhActionPhaseAnim()`. Even if the active attack row is new, late recovery can flash old LAMUH.
3. `down_heavy_special` and `up_heavy_special` use redesigned Sheet 3 active rows, but their startup/recovery phase logic can call `crown_charge` / `crown_recovery`, which are old fallback aliases while `LAMUH_ASCENDED_BODY_ATLAS_ENABLED` is false.
4. Direct legacy aliases `special_2` and `special_3` still map to old Sheet 5 rows in the static animation table. Runtime phase logic normally redirects them to Mirror Pulse / Crown Beam, but debug/direct animation calls can still show old art.
5. Air specials are not redesigned: Air Mirror Spark uses old Sheet 5 Row 0, Air Dash Strike/Radiant Dive use old Sheet 5 Row 4, and Air Crown Drop reuses old air-heavy art.
6. Crouch and directional normals still use old ground-normal rows. Standing J/K/L equivalents are redesigned, but crouch/forward/back/launcher variants are not.

## Missing Bespoke Rows

- Critical: Defense/reactions redesign rows for stand block, crouch block, air block, light hit, medium hit, heavy hit/launch, air hit, knockdown, downed, and get-up.
- Critical: Shared redesigned special recovery row so Sheet 3/4/forward-special moves do not recover into old Sheet 5 art.
- High: Air specials redesign rows for Air Mirror Spark, Air Dash Strike, and Air Crown Drop.
- High: Crouch/directional normal redesign rows for crouch light/medium/heavy, launcher, forward light/medium/heavy, and back light/medium/heavy.
- High: Core movement cleanup rows for walk back, dash back, crouch, jump, fall, air dash, and landing.
- High: Super/ascended body continuity rows for super idle, activation, attack overlay, and recovery using the same redesigned LAMUH identity.
- Medium: End/flavor redesign rows for KO, intro, victory, level-up, and taunt.

## Recommended Next Sprite Work

Next target should be a LAMUH Defense + Reactions + Knockdown/Get-up redesign sheet, before another offensive special sheet. Those states trigger constantly whenever LAMUH blocks or gets hit, and they currently account for the most obvious old-style gameplay flashes. After that, target Air Specials/Air Normals and crouch/directional normals.
