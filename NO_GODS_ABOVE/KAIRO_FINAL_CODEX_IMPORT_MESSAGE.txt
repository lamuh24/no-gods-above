Rules:
- every sheet is 6 columns x 5 rows
- slice every sheet uniformly
- use bottom-center anchoring
- align grounded animations to one shared ground baseline
- do not treat sprite-internal pose shifts as world movement
- keep dash / projectile / special travel handled in code
- keep the build runnable at every step
