# No Gods Above Visual Rules

These rules guard the visual identity for supervised agent work.

## Direction

- No Gods Above is a 2.5D anime fighting game.
- The house tone is dark ink, cathedral red, blood red, brass, old gold, and eclipse shadow.
- Visuals should feel sharp, readable, dramatic, and game-ready rather than generic prompt art.
- Preserve the current roster silhouettes, outfits, skin tones, hair, weapons, motifs, and body scale.
- Character art generation requires exact repo references first.

## Rejection Rules

- No generic anime redesigns.
- No blind character generation.
- No photorealism.
- No watermarks.
- No fake text baked into images.
- No extra characters in frame unless the approved task explicitly requests them.
- No unlisted characters from `agent/memory/character_truth.md`.
- No final Steam/key art with characters until character references are exported and validated.
- Do not mix portrait styles without a declared task.
- Do not prioritize health bar or meter replacement. The user already has health bars, and they must not be replaced unless explicitly requested.

## Asset Handling

- Generated art must land in a review area first, never directly into `NO_GODS_ABOVE/assets/`.
- Character-dependent art requests with no exact reference paths are invalid.
- Final or 4K generation requires Claude validation and human approval before spend.
- Transparent PNGs are required where runtime sprites, overlays, UI cuts, or VFX need alpha.
- Sprite work must preserve baselines, pivots, anchors, and runtime cell contracts.
- Do not move generated art into live assets until it has been normalized, validated, and approved.
- Do not delete existing assets during visual work unless a human explicitly approves the removal.

## Character Preservation

- Kairo stays the cyan cyber blade striker.
- Vanta stays the crimson rival.
- Nyx stays aerial rushdown with phantom slash language.
- Sol stays the iron sun brawler.
- Seris stays halo-chain/midrange pressure using the current `seris_revamp_final` source.
- LAMUH stays the Black male celestial ki martial artist with locs, white haori, gold/cyan accents, and body-first combat.
- LAMUH Legacy stays the classic LAMUH variant.
- Celeste stays the seven-spirit trickster/conductor.
- Sable, when referenced, is gated/not-current-playable and uses placeholder/test art until an approved reference-gated art task exists.
